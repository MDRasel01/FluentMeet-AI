# Step 09 — Pronunciation Improvement Exercises & Minimal Pairs

## 1. Overview & Pedagogical Method

The `PronunciationRecommendationGenerator` transforms detected acoustic weaknesses into structured, actionable pronunciation exercises. Rather than presenting abstract linguistic critiques, the engine provides high-yield drills:
- Minimal pair contrasts
- Vowel lengthening and reduction drills
- Syllable stress placement exercises
- Conversational pacing drills

---

## 2. Minimal Pair Exercise Catalog

Minimal pair practice isolates the single contrasting phoneme that alters lexical meaning, retraining muscle memory and airflow:

### 2.1 Voiceless Dental Fricative (/θ/ vs /s/, /t/)
- **Trigger**: Detected substitution of `/t/` or `/s/` for `"TH"` (e.g. in `"think"`, `"three"`, `"third"`).
- **Instruction**: Gently rest tongue tip between upper and lower front teeth. Maintain continuous exhalation friction rather than stopping airflow.
- **Drill Pairs**:
  - `think / sink`
  - `three / tree`
  - `thin / tin`
  - `thought / taught`

### 2.2 Voiced Dental Fricative (/ð/ vs /d/)
- **Trigger**: Substitution of `/d/` for `"DH"` in `"they"`, `"then"`, `"there"`.
- **Drill Pairs**:
  - `they / day`
  - `then / den`
  - `there / dare`
  - `breathe / breed`

### 2.3 Labiodental Fricative (/v/ vs /w/)
- **Trigger**: Bilabial rounding when articulating labiodental `"V"` in `"very"`, `"value"`, `"review"`.
- **Drill Pairs**:
  - `very / wary`
  - `vine / wine`
  - `vest / west`
  - `value / whale`

---

## 3. Prioritization & Difficulty Tiers

Exercises are assigned priorities based on communication impact:
1. **High Priority**: Phonetic substitutions that alter semantic meaning (e.g., *three* vs *tree*), or extreme speaking pace ($> 180\text{ WPM}$).
2. **Medium Priority**: Consonant cluster reductions or minor vowel shortening.
3. **Low Priority**: Expressive pitch range enhancement for monotone speakers.
